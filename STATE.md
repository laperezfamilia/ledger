- **New (2026-08-02): a ninth methodological observation, Tacit Expertise & Embodied Judgment.** Discovery kept forcing single explicit reasons for Beto's photography decisions (e.g. "why did he lower the camera?"); the founder pushed back — "artists have an eye... they know the moment" — rejecting the assumption that meaningful behavior always traces through consciously articulated reasoning. Possible adaptation: replace forced "why" questions with ones that invite articulation without assuming reasoning precedes judgment ("what told him this wasn't the right moment?"). Open question: should this become its own evidence category, or stay a specialized form of observation? Working methodology only, requires testing across additional characters and non-artistic domains (teachers, physicians, musicians, craftspeople, athletes, parents, experienced leaders).
