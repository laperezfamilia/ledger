# Casa Pérez Educational Brief v1.0 (Recovered Working Draft)

**Research Program:** Educational Philosophy

**Status:** Recovered Working Draft

**Source:** Educational Philosophy Discovery Thread

**Purpose:** Recovered artifact intended to serve as the foundation for the future canonical Educational Brief. This document preserves discoveries made during educational philosophy exploration. It should not be interpreted as the final Educational Philosophy or the final Educational Architecture.

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document reconstructs the Educational Brief that emerged organically from this discussion.

It is not an Educational Constitution.

It is not the complete Educational Philosophy.

It is not the Curriculum Architecture.

Its purpose is to define the educational foundation required before launching Sofía's YouTube channel and to establish the educational assumptions currently supported by this thread.

It intentionally separates:

- observations,
- founder decisions,
- working hypotheses,
- working theses,
- established philosophy,
- and unresolved research.

Only ideas that genuinely emerged during this discussion are included.

---

## 1. Educational Purpose

**Observation**

The original discussion began as planning for Sofía's YouTube channel.

It gradually revealed that the channel required more than entertaining scripts.

Repeatedly, the discussion returned to the need for:

- educational coherence
- instructional integrity
- cumulative learning
- a defined learner journey

rather than disconnected educational content.

**Confidence:** High

---

**Founder Decision**

Sofía is not intended to become:

"a fun character who occasionally teaches Spanish."

She is intended to become:

"a believable Spanish teacher."

This distinction changes the design requirements of the channel.

Teaching decisions should be educationally defensible rather than simply engaging.

**Status:** Founder Decision

---

**Working Thesis**

The channel should gradually help learners participate more comfortably in ordinary life through Spanish rather than merely accumulate vocabulary or grammar knowledge.

This repeatedly appeared as the educational direction of the channel.

It has not yet been operationalized or tested.

**Status:** Working Thesis

---

## 2. Educational Promise

**Observation**

Jamie repeatedly described the desired learner experience as:

- confidence
- participation
- understanding
- strategy
- enjoyable practice
- meaningful repetition

rather than memorization.

**Confidence:** High

---

**Established Philosophy**

Conversation remains central to Casa Pérez.

Previously established philosophy:

Conversation is the product. Language learning is the outcome.

Nothing in this discussion contradicted that philosophy.

**Status:** Established Philosophy

Participation may eventually become the organizing principle of the curriculum.

However:

This discussion concluded that participation has not yet been defined rigorously enough to function as curriculum architecture.

Further research is required.

**Status:** Working Hypothesis

## 3. Educational Integrity

**Founder Decision**

Educational decisions should always be explainable.

Jamie wants every instructional choice to have a reason that can be articulated.

Examples include:

- Why this concept?
- Why now?
- Why taught this way?
- What does it prepare learners for?
- What educational research supports the decision?
- How will the learner encounter it again later?

Teaching should not rely on intuition alone.

**Status:** Founder Decision

---

## 4. Educational Research Sources

Casa Pérez intentionally studies existing educational systems without reproducing them.

Educational sources are studied to understand principles rather than products.

Guiding principle:

We study educational principles, not educational products.

## 5. Educational Scope

**Founder Decision**

The Educational Brief is channel-specific.

It does not attempt to define the educational architecture of the entire Casa Pérez ecosystem.

The larger Educational Philosophy remains a separate research program.

**Status:** Founder Decision

---

**Observation**

The discussion repeatedly distinguished four layers.

These should remain separate.

---

**Educational Philosophy**

Why we teach the way we do.

---

**Educational Architecture**

How that philosophy expresses itself across products.

---

**Curriculum Architecture**

What is taught. In what sequence. Why.

---

**Sofía's Teaching Philosophy**

How Sofía naturally teaches because of who she is.

These layers should not be merged into one document.

**Confidence:** High

## 6. Relationship Between Character and Teaching

**Founder Decision**

Sofía's teaching philosophy must emerge from her character.

Teaching should not be imposed for product convenience.

Character precedes pedagogy.

**Status:** Founder Decision

---

**Observation**

The discussion repeatedly concluded:

We cannot honestly determine how Sofía teaches until Sofía herself is fully understood.

This became one of the strongest recurring themes.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 7. Product Definition Before Launch

**Founder Decision**

The channel should not launch on the basis of several isolated exploratory scripts.

Instead, the required sequence is:

1. Complete Sofía's character development.
2. Conduct educational research.
3. Create the Educational Brief.
4. Develop a provisional instructional spine.
5. Produce a small, testable launch batch.

**Status:** Founder Direction

---

**Observation**

The strongest supporting argument was:

Whatever publishes first becomes the de facto opening curriculum.

Therefore:

The instructional spine is not premature abstraction.

It is product definition.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 8. Instructional Spine

**Founder Decision**

Before producing a large number of Shorts, the channel should possess a provisional instructional spine.

Purpose:

To ensure cumulative learning rather than disconnected educational content.

**Status:** Founder Direction

---

**Working Direction**

Current planning target:

Approximately 12–15 concept-level entries.

Not 12–15 completed scripts.

This recommendation emerged during discussion with Claude.

It remains provisional.

---

**Observation**

The instructional spine should map educational progression rather than completed content.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 9. Initial Content Planning

**Founder Direction**

The launch batch should remain intentionally small.

Current recommendation:

Approximately 3–5 Shorts.

They should be selected from the instructional spine rather than from exploratory scripts unless those scripts naturally belong within the mapped progression.

**Status:** Founder Direction

- Who is the learner?

- What can a 15–30 second Short realistically accomplish?

## Closing Assessment

This discussion did not produce a complete Educational Brief.

It did produce the foundation that an Educational Brief requires.

Most importantly, it established that the Brief is not intended to answer every educational question.

Its role is narrower and more practical:

To define the educational identity, learner promise, research stance, instructional intent, and launch assumptions of Sofía's YouTube channel before curriculum design and content production begin.

The broader Educational Philosophy and Educational Architecture remain active research programs whose conclusions should mature through evidence rather than assumption.
