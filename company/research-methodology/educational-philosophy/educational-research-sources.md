# Educational Research Sources (Proposed)

**Status: Proposed addition, not canon.** Submitted by Jamie & Solara for discussion and refinement — not yet adopted as part of this program's working methodology.

**Scope note:** this file captures only the "Educational Research Sources" section as originally transmitted, and stands as a complete artifact in its own right (confirmed by Jamie, 2026-08-02). It is **not** the full Educational Brief — that has since been recovered separately at `./educational-brief-v1.0-recovered.md`, whose §4 ("Educational Research Sources") covers the same three sources independently, as part of the larger recovered draft. The two are kept as distinct artifacts rather than merged; this file is not superseded by the Brief.

## Why this is a distinct section, not a list of inspirations

Jamie's framing, preserved as stated: this is not a list of favorite teachers. It is a research corpus — the educational systems, educators, methodologies, and research traditions Casa Pérez intentionally studies while developing its own products.

**Guiding principle (proposed):**

> Casa Pérez studies a wide range of language-learning methodologies, educators, and instructional approaches to understand why they are effective. These sources inform our thinking but do not serve as templates for our curriculum, lessons, products, or teaching materials. We study educational principles, not educational products.

**Proposed research principle for the Shared Research Philosophy (`../README.md`):**

> We study educational principles, not educational products.

Noted as a candidate guardrail as the project grows and new educational influences are encountered — not yet added to `../README.md`'s "Shared Research Philosophy" list, pending discussion.

## Current research sources

### Language Transfer

Current reasons for study:
- Reasoning rather than memorization
- Helping adults understand how the language works
- English used as productive scaffolding
- Learner confidence through understanding
- Respect for adult intelligence
- Progression that often prioritizes cognitive leverage over rigid textbook sequencing

### Dreaming Spanish

Current reasons for study:
- Comprehensible input
- Natural language acquisition through understandable content
- Extensive listening
- Lowering learner stress
- Increasing meaningful exposure
- Allowing intuition to develop through repeated understandable experiences

### Qroo Paul

Current reasons for study:
- Instructional organization
- Sequencing of ideas
- Practical explanation style
- Helping learners connect concepts rather than memorize isolated facts

**Research status:** needs additional structured study before drawing stronger conclusions.

## Treatment of sources

**Important — these should not become "models to combine."** Each source is treated as an independent origin of educational principles to evaluate on its own terms, not raw material for a composite curriculum.

For every educational principle Casa Pérez eventually adopts, the proposed evaluation questions are:
- What makes this effective?
- What educational problem is it solving?
- What evidence supports it?
- Does it align with the Casa Pérez philosophy?
- Does it fit Sofía naturally as a teacher?
- Does it fit a 15–30 second Short?
- Does it fit the broader learner journey?
- If adopted, how would Casa Pérez express this principle in its own original way?

## Relationship to existing approaches

Casa Pérez is not attempting to replace Language Transfer, Dreaming Spanish, Qroo Paul, or other language-education approaches — per Jamie, these are making valuable, distinct contributions to adult language education already.

**Emerging hypothesis (not yet evidence-backed):** Casa Pérez may contribute something unique around helping learners participate more comfortably in ordinary life through Spanish, while learning from the strengths of multiple educational traditions. This connects to, but is not identical to, `../README.md`'s Research Program 2 working hypothesis (belonging, meaningful interaction, contextual learning, and psychologically safe practice) and Research Program 4's Casa Pérez hypothesis (can belonging precede learning?) — noted as a cross-program connection, not merged into either.
