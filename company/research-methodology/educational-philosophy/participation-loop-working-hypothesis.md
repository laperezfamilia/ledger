# The Participation Loop — Working Hypothesis (Discovery Only)

**Status: mixed — see the Founder Decision below for what's settled.** Most of this document remains Discovery / working hypothesis, not canon. One section is a real Founder Decision (2026-08-06): *whether* Casa Pérez designs around participation + specific relational Witness is resolved. *How* to execute it well remains open research — that distinction governs the whole file from here.

## Founder Decision (2026-08-06): Casa Pérez will be designed around Participation + Specific Relational Witness

**This is settled, not provisional.** Casa Pérez creates abundant, natural opportunities for learners to participate in the life of the world, rather than positioning speaking as something earned after sufficient instruction. Participation may begin extremely small and grow naturally. When meaningful growth becomes visible through an ongoing relationship, characters may occasionally name something specific and true they've noticed.

**The approved design model:**

> Relationship gives participation meaning. Courage makes participation possible before confidence exists. Participation creates lived evidence of capability. Witness helps the learner recognize that evidence. Recognition can strengthen confidence and identity. Greater confidence makes further participation easier.

**Explicitly not a reward mechanic — this is the part most worth protecting from drift.** *Participation → reward* is the wrong model, rejected deliberately. The model is *participation → lived experience → growth becomes visible → someone who knows you notices.* Characters do not dispense praise to reinforce behavior; they notice because they are in relationship. Recognition/Witness must remain: specific, truthful, relationally plausible, rare, earned, human-scale rather than dashboard-like. The purpose is not evaluation or manufactured motivation — it is to stand witness to who the learner is becoming.

**What this Founder Decision does and doesn't resolve, stated precisely:** it settles that Casa Pérez builds both participation opportunities and relational Witness. It does **not** resolve whether Witness adds unique causal value beyond participation alone, or whether relationship-continuity specifically (versus generic warmth) is what makes Witness work — those remain open research questions, listed below, now reframed as *how to execute well* rather than *whether to do this at all*. The decision to proceed was made on Jamie's judgment and the accumulated Discovery trail, not because those causal questions were answered — worth being honest about that distinction rather than implying more certainty than exists.

## The core hypothesis

> Language becomes usable through participation, not merely acquisition. Casa Pérez creates a place where learners can participate before they feel ready.

**The working loop:**

> Belonging → Courage → Participation → Evidence → Witness/Recognition → Confidence/Identity → More Participation

More humanly: *Know → Dare → Participate → Be witnessed → Participate again → Become.*

**The formulation Jamie & Solara most want preserved:**

> Stop making speaking the graduation ceremony. Make speaking where learning happens.

**Explicit scope note, preserved as stated:** this doesn't devalue vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, curriculum, explicit teaching, or structured practice. The narrower question: *what prevents acquired language knowledge from becoming usable participation, and can belonging, low-stakes relational interaction, courage, and specific recognition help learners cross that gap?*

## Why this emerged

Converging from several directions independently: learners reporting years or decades of study while still feeling unable to speak; the language communities Jamie has studied being praised specifically for members who actually speak, fear mistakes less, interact with similar-level peers, discuss ordinary life, and participate in supportive community; and the observation that Casa Pérez's own product design already produces this without anyone intending a "practice session" — a learner participates because Beto said something funny or Rosa asked a question, not because they decided to practice Spanish.

**Natural scaling, as described:** 😂 → jajaja → sí → sí, yo también → short phrase → complete response → story → initiating a conversation.

## Where this already intersects existing work

Not a new idea arriving in isolation — it's a mechanism-level connector between several things already established separately:

- **`../design-research/recognition-vs-evaluation.md`** ("the purpose of data is not judgment, the purpose of data is witness") explained *why* witnessing matters. This hypothesis adds *what* should be witnessed specifically (a shift in participation, not general performance) and *why* witnessing that particular thing has causal power — it converts evidence into identity, not just morale.
- **`./educational-brief-v1.0-recovered.md` §1**'s Working Thesis ("the channel should help learners participate more comfortably in ordinary life through Spanish") — this hypothesis is a mechanism-level elaboration of that exact thesis, not a competing one.
- **`./conversational-immersion-observation-2026-08-02.md`** — read retrospectively, that observation (Jamie speaking Spanish unprompted mid-conversation) is arguably a lived instance of this loop already firing: belonging present, courage exercised without anyone requesting it, participation occurring inside a real relationship.
- **`../character-architecture/experiments/sofia-teaching-philosophy.md`** — "mistakes are evidence learning is already happening," "she listens before she corrects" — already describes a character oriented around exactly this loop, without it having been named as a loop.
- **The engagement-ethics work in `../design-research/`** (payoff matches pull, the transparency test, real-world-participation as the third test) — directly relevant to keeping participation-opportunity design honest; see the opportunities-vs-measurement distinction below.

**Is this a renaming of something already established, or genuinely new?** Judged as genuinely new, but not independent: it fills in mechanism-level detail that the existing "Mechanism" layer (a psychologically safe-to-speak environment built through consistent relationships) left abstract. Confidence Transfer names the *outcome*; the Participation Loop proposes the *engine* that produces it. Worth watching whether this becomes a sixth name for the same underlying cluster (Practice Family, Bridge Not Destination, Safe-to-Speak, Confidence Transfer already exist) or whether it earns its place as the mechanism layer specifically — not resolved here.

## Distinguishing participation opportunities from participation measurement

The real risk this hypothesis creates, named directly rather than left implicit: designing generous **opportunities** for participation is squarely aligned with everything already established. Building **measurement** infrastructure to track participation is where this could quietly become the gamification system Jamie & Solara are explicitly trying to avoid.

**Proposed working discipline:** witnessing should be triggered by what a believable, attentive family member could plausibly notice from memory over time — not by a counter, threshold, or dashboard. The test: *if stating the observed shift accurately would require consulting a log rather than genuine memory, it has crossed into surveillance-flavored territory, regardless of whether the underlying data exists.* Sofía's example works ("hace unas semanas me contestabas casi siempre en inglés") because it's the shape of thing a real person remembers — coarse, episodic, meaningful — not the shape of a metrics export.

A second discipline worth naming: witnessing moments should stay **rare and earned**, not frequent or systematic. The moment witnessing becomes predictable or scheduled, it degrades back into evaluation — the exact thing `recognition-vs-evaluation.md` already exists to prevent.

## Relationship as mechanism, not just environment — pressure-tested

Asked directly whether relationship is part of the educational mechanism itself, not merely the emotional scaffolding around it: the Witness step specifically appears to require it. A generic, warm, memory-less chatbot cannot perform Sofía's example line — there is no "hace unas semanas" without continuity of a specific relationship. If the Participation Loop holds, relationship-with-continuity is mechanically necessary for Witness specifically, not just pleasant scaffolding around acquisition elsewhere in the loop. This is falsifiable: if a generic, non-continuous source of equally warm encouragement produced equally strong identity-shift outcomes, that would argue against relationship being constitutive. Directly connects to, and would be real evidence toward, the still-open Character Architecture question of whether character-specific continuity outperforms generic AI.

## Testable through the product vs. needs outside research first — updated 2026-08-06, desk research now done

**The desk research step is complete.** Jamie's independent literature review confirmed what the applied-linguistics literature already establishes: the broad claim that participation, belonging, and low-stakes relational interaction reduce the anxiety/performance gap and increase willingness to communicate is well-supported, existing territory — not a novel Casa Pérez discovery. Relevant established lines: Krashen's Affective Filter Hypothesis, MacIntyre's Willingness to Communicate model, Horwitz's Foreign Language Classroom Anxiety Scale, and the communicative-competence-versus-linguistic-competence distinction (Hymes, contra a narrower Chomskyan competence). This sharpens rather than closes the hypothesis: it means the design decision to build around participation rests on solid ground, and the genuinely open territory is narrower and more specific than originally framed.

**What's now known vs. what's still frontier:** established literature does not specifically address (a) whether relationship *continuity* with a specific character — as opposed to generic warmth or a new interlocutor each time — meaningfully changes willingness to communicate or participation outcomes, or (b) whether explicit Witness/Recognition adds causal value beyond the participation opportunity itself. Both are now the actual research frontier, reframed by the 2026-08-06 Founder Decision above as *how to execute this well*, not *whether to do it*.

**Testable directly through the product, once something exists:** whether specific participation-inviting design choices increase attempted Spanish use; whether witnessing moments produce any observable behavior change afterward (via the self-report/real-world-missions infrastructure already planned); whether continuity of relationship (same character, remembered history) measurably outperforms generic warmth on participation or WTC-style measures.

## Smallest set of high-value research questions before designing around this heavily — narrowed 2026-08-06

Desk research (former question 1) is done — see above. Two questions remain, both now framed as execution research rather than go/no-go research:

1. **Continuity:** does relationship continuity specifically — a particular character who remembers you, versus generic or rotating warmth — measurably affect willingness to communicate or participation, beyond what generic encouragement would produce?
2. **Witness:** does Witness/Recognition specifically add anything causally beyond Participation alone — or would repeated low-stakes participation, without any explicit external witnessing, produce the same identity shift on its own?

A third, more practical question stays live alongside these: **can the human-scale-noticing boundary be operationalized reliably**, or does it require case-by-case judgment that resists systematization? Testable cheaply via early mockups and small user tests, before any real build commitment.

**A caution flagged on the "people who date a native speaker learn faster" folk observation Jamie encountered while researching continuity:** treat it as an interesting lead, not evidence — it's likely confounded by survivorship bias (people who were already more willing to participate are also more likely to enter and sustain that kind of relationship) and by stakes/immersion volume, not necessarily continuity in isolation. Worth keeping in mind so it doesn't get smuggled in as support for the continuity question above.

**A natural, unmanufactured place to observe the continuity and Witness questions without building a dedicated experiment:** Find the Words and any future Casa Pérez "family chat"-style surface will generate real usage data on relationship continuity by default, simply by existing. Worth watching that data once it exists rather than engineering a separate study.

## Consequentiality — a further Discovery note (2026-08-06, explicitly Discovery, not part of the Founder Decision above)

A related but distinct idea, surfaced the same day as the Founder Decision above but **deliberately kept out of it, per Jamie's explicit instruction** — this is Discovery, a lead worth tracking, not a settled design commitment:

> Practice asks for an answer. Participation allows your answer to matter.

The observation: participation may matter more when it's *consequential* — when a character has a genuine stake in the learner's answer — rather than purely low-stakes rehearsal. Illustrative shape (not yet built, not yet specified in detail): Rosa caring which dress the learner thinks she should wear, Beto wanting to know what the learner thinks of a photo he took, Sofía genuinely wanting the learner's opinion on something. In each case the learner's answer is requested because someone in the fiction actually wants to know, not because it's a drill.

**An explicit non-coercion boundary, stated directly and worth protecting carefully:** we do not want characters manufacturing guilt, disappointment, withdrawal of affection, jealousy, or other coercive stakes to drive engagement. Consequentiality, if pursued, has to stay on the same side of the line the rest of this hypothesis already protects — genuine relational interest, not manufactured pressure.

**How this relates to the two narrowed research questions above:** Consequentiality is plausibly a third variable, separate from both Continuity and Witness — worth keeping distinct rather than folding into either, until there's reason to believe otherwise. It may turn out to be what makes continuity or Witness work, or it may be independent of both.

## Open questions (Discovery, not resolved)

- Does Witness add causal power beyond Participation alone, or is repeated low-stakes participation sufficient by itself?
- Does relationship continuity specifically (a remembered, specific character) outperform generic warmth on participation or willingness-to-communicate outcomes?
- Is "Participation" a genuinely distinct mechanism-layer concept, or will it fold into the existing Confidence Transfer / Mechanism language once pressure-tested?
- Two learners with similar vocabulary and grammar may have radically different real-world readiness if one has repeatedly participated, misunderstood, repaired, initiated, persisted, and been understood. If true, traditional language measures may capture something different from the capability Casa Pérez actually cares about — not yet tested.
- **Consequentiality (2026-08-06, Discovery, explicitly not part of the Founder Decision above):** does a character having a genuine stake in the learner's answer add motivational or acquisition value beyond low-stakes participation alone — and can this be pursued without crossing into the manufactured-stakes territory explicitly ruled out (guilt, disappointment, withdrawal of affection, jealousy)?
