# Company Research Programs (Working Draft)

**Status: Working Research Questions — Not Canon**

These research questions organize the company's long-term research efforts. They are questions, not conclusions. They should guide evidence collection, experimentation, and product development without assuming the answers in advance.

This document is the high-level map. Over time, each research program can grow into its own folder with evidence, experiments, hypotheses, and replications (as Program 1 already has, at `./character-architecture/`) — this file stays the index, not the evidence itself.

---

## Research Program 1 — Character Architecture

**Research Question:** Can psychologically generative fictional characters be discovered through a repeatable process rather than traditional top-down character design?

**Current Status:**
- Working research question
- Evidence collection in progress
- Initial evidence: Sofía (Experiment 1)
- Next major replication: Beto (Experiment 2)

**Working Hypothesis:** Psychologically coherent characters become increasingly generative when they are discovered through observation, hypothesis, calibration, and lived behavior rather than assembled from personality traits.

**Program folder:** `./character-architecture/`

**Current Status:**

**Current Status:**

**Research Question:** Can recognition-based learning accelerate language acquisition more effectively than traditional drill-based practice?

**Current Status:**
- Prototype testing in progress

**Working Hypothesis:** Recognizing meaningful real-world situations and relationships may strengthen language acquisition more effectively than isolated vocabulary memorization or repetitive drills.

**Research Question:** What human truths do existing products reveal, and how would Casa Pérez naturally express those same truths in its own way?

## Shared Research Philosophy

Across all research programs, the company strives to preserve the same standards:

- Questions before conclusions.
- Evidence before doctrine.
- Observation before interpretation.
- Replication before generalization.
- Honest uncertainty over premature certainty.
- Products serve as experiments.
- Research guides products; products generate new research.

The objective is not to prove existing beliefs correct. The objective is to discover what is true.
