# Research Log — Character Architecture

Chronological journal. Discoveries recorded here **as observations, not doctrine.** Interpretation, confidence level, and replication status belong in `./evidence-index.md`, not here — this log is the raw record of what was noticed and when.

**Provenance note:** the entries below were supplied by Jamie & Solara as examples of the kind of discovery this log exists to hold, at the program's founding (2026-08-01). ## Seed entries (2026-08-01, source: Sofía discovery)

- **Attention precedes dialogue.** Before consistent dialogue can be written for a character, what they consistently *notice* has to be understood first. (Connects to `casa-perez/characters/character-voice-methodology.md`'s "Dialogue Is Downstream of Attention" principle — same discovery, documented in two places for two different purposes: that file states it as applied character-voice methodology; this log records it as a character-architecture research observation.)
- **Ordinary Tuesdays reveal more character than dramatic scenarios.** Small, unremarkable moments appear to expose character more reliably than high-stakes ones.
- **Behaviors reveal character more reliably than adjectives.** Naming a trait ("she's kind") explains less than observing what she actually does.
- **Character becomes increasingly generative as constitutional coherence improves.** As the underlying rules governing a character become more internally consistent, the character appears to generate more material on their own, requiring less invented-from-scratch authoring.
- **Continuous micro-calibration significantly improves psychological consistency.** Small, frequent corrections appear to outperform large, infrequent ones at keeping a character's psychology coherent over time.

## 2026-08-01 — Cross-domain observation: concrete example over abstract description (not yet elevated to methodology)

The same underlying instinct appears to have emerged independently in three separate places, before anyone named it as a pattern: (1) this program's own "behaviors reveal character more reliably than adjectives" finding; (2) Agrupa's Dialogue Approval Rule, requiring any proposed line of Sofía's dialogue to be reviewed as an actual quotation rather than approved as an abstract description of intended effect (`casa-perez/projects/agrupa/copy-library.md`); (3) the discovery, during the 2026-08-01 Sofía synthesis session, that architectural principles become significantly more durable when anchored to lived scenes. **Explicitly not elevated into methodology** — Jamie & Solara were clear this observation alone doesn't warrant that. It was, however, judged strong enough evidence to justify a documentation-convention change: see `../README.md`'s "Documentation Convention (Approved 2026-08-01)" — Principle → Canonical Example(s) → Interpretation, now the working standard for future Character Architecture handoffs.
