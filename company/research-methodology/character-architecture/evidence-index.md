# Evidence Index — Character Architecture

For each major discovery: Observation, Supporting evidence, Counter-evidence (when available), Current confidence level, Future replication requirements. Every significant claim should connect to evidence, not intuition alone. Entries here elaborate on `./research-log.md`'s raw entries — the log is *what was noticed*, this index is *how much we trust it and why*.

### Attention precedes dialogue

- **Observation:** Consistent dialogue for a character requires first understanding what they consistently notice — attention is a more stable layer than vocabulary, cadence, or humor.
- **Supporting evidence:** Sofía discovery (the "Dialogue Is Downstream of Attention" finding — see `casa-perez/characters/character-voice-methodology.md`).
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Low-Moderate — single-character observation, not yet replicated.
- **Future replication requirements:** Confirm during Beto's independent discovery; test against at least one non-Casa-Pérez character.

### Ordinary Tuesdays reveal more character than dramatic scenarios

- **Observation:** Small, unremarkable moments expose character more reliably than high-stakes scenes.
- **Supporting evidence:** Sofía discovery (general pattern, not tied to one specific document currently in `ledger`).
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Low — asserted, not yet tested against a counter-case (a dramatic scenario that *did* reveal strong character).
- **Future replication requirements:** Deliberately test a dramatic-scenario case against an ordinary-moment case for the same character.

### Behaviors reveal character more reliably than adjectives

- **Observation:** What a character does explains them better than naming a trait about them.
- **Supporting evidence:** Sofía discovery; consistent with the Human Recognition Diagnostic's Bucket A/B/C test (`casa-perez/characters/character-voice-methodology.md`) — behavior/causation is the passing case, trait-labeling is not tested there directly but the underlying instinct matches.
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Moderate — consistent with a second, independently-developed diagnostic (the Human Recognition Diagnostic), which is a mild form of replication even though both trace back to the same character (Sofía).
- **Future replication requirements:** Test against Beto; test against a non-Casa-Pérez character.

### Character becomes increasingly generative as constitutional coherence improves

- **Observation:** As the rules governing a character become more internally consistent, the character produces more material with less invented-from-scratch authoring.
- **Supporting evidence:** Sofía discovery, general pattern.
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Low — no defined measure of "generative" or "constitutional coherence" yet; currently qualitative impression.
- **Future replication requirements:** Define what "generative" would mean operationally before this can be tested rather than just asserted.

### Continuous micro-calibration significantly improves psychological consistency

- **Observation:** Small, frequent corrections outperform large, infrequent ones at keeping a character psychologically coherent over time.
- **Supporting evidence:** Sofía discovery, general pattern.
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Low — no comparison case (a character developed via large, infrequent corrections) exists yet to test against.
- **Future replication requirements:** Would need a deliberate comparison, or emergence of the same pattern unprompted during Beto's discovery.

### Integrity & Dignity as a family-level organizing pattern

- **Observation:** Sofía's behaviors are better explained by integrity and dignity than generic warmth/kindness language; hypothesized to extend to Carmen and Rosa with different expressions.
- **Supporting evidence:** Full record at `casa-perez/research/family-architecture-integrity-dignity.md`.
- **Counter-evidence:** None recorded yet.
- **Current confidence:** Low-Moderate — coherent explanatory fit for one character (Sofía), extended to two more (Carmen, Rosa) by inference/pattern-matching, not independent discovery.
- **Future replication requirements:** Explicitly named validation point — must emerge unsteered during Beto's discovery to meaningfully raise confidence.
