# Experiment 1 — Sofía Pérez

**Status: Discovery Complete → Stewardship (2026-08-02).** Character Architecture Reference v1.0 is established as the founder-authorized primary reference. Per the team operating model (`company/governance/operating-model.md`), this experiment has transitioned out of Discovery — **future changes require new evidence rather than speculative refinement.** This does not mean the reference is frozen forever; it means changes from here are evidence-driven (a new observation, a real inconsistency surfacing in use, a finding from Beto's experiment), not incremental polish for its own sake. Sofía is the founding case for this research program — most current entries in `../research-log.md` and `../evidence-index.md` trace back to her discovery process.

**Lifecycle stage (per `../README.md`'s Proposed Character Architecture Experiment Lifecycle, working observation):** Experiment Created → Discovery Active → Discovery Complete → Reference Established → **Stewardship (current)**. ## What this experiment is testing

Whether the discoveries made through Sofía's development (attention-first character work, behavior-over-adjective description, micro-calibration, the integrity/dignity pattern) represent a repeatable *process*, or are specific to how Sofía in particular was developed.

## Current role in the research program

Sofía is currently the **only** fully-developed data point. Every discovery in `../evidence-index.md` is rated low-to-moderate confidence specifically because it has not yet been replicated against a second character. Experiment 2 (Beto) is the next planned test — see `../README.md` and `../open-questions.md`.
