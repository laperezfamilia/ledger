# Experiment 2 — Beto Pérez

**Status: Discovery Active (2026-08-02).** Character Architecture's second experiment has begun — the first real test of nearly every working thesis, hypothesis, and lifecycle model this program has proposed so far (the Discovery/Preservation split, the Founder-Approved Working Thesis, the Proposed Character Architecture Experiment Lifecycle, "characters teach because they love differently," and the Documentation Convention itself). This experiment session is also where the Working Thesis itself was revised and Founder-approved — see `../README.md`.

**Lifecycle stage (per `../README.md`'s Proposed Character Architecture Experiment Lifecycle, working observation):** Experiment Created → **Discovery Active (current)**. No Reference has been established yet — this is the first session.

**Dual purpose, stated explicitly by Jamie & Solara:** Experiment 2 exists to (1) discover Beto Pérez, and (2) stress-test the Character Architecture methodology itself. Every major insight is evaluated on two levels — what it reveals about Beto, and what it reveals about Character Architecture as a discipline. This is a deliberate shift from Experiment 1, which discovered Sofía without simultaneously auditing the methodology used to discover her.

**Primary reference:** none founder-approved yet. A first draft now exists — `./beto-character-architecture-reference-DRAFT-checkpoint-1.md`, Checkpoint 1, two principles (Attention, Human Dignity), explicitly not a Version 1.0 and not founder-approved. Built by Claude acting as architectural steward over a Discovery synthesis Jamie & Solara handed over directly, per their explicit request to lean into role specialization — organize, weigh evidence, preserve traceability, shape into the established format, without inventing or filling gaps. Expected to prompt another round of Discovery before anything here is authorized.

## What's here so far

- **`./beto-2026-08-02-methodology-stress-test.md`** (Discovery Session 1) — the first Beto discovery session, recorded with its dual character/methodology framing intact. Contains the first Beto-specific evidence — an outward-orientation organizing principle that drifted through several increasingly reductive interpretations before founder correction, the emerging Occupation/Calling/Orientation distinction, and Beto's dream of becoming an internationally-published traveling photographer/videographer, which turned out to express a worldview formed by travel (universal truths crossing cultural barriers, learning to celebrate difference) rather than being the source of that worldview — plus eleven proposed methodological observations for Character Architecture itself, none founder-approved as canonical methodology yet. The eighth, Vertical Inquiry ("Why?"), was flagged as possibly the most significant observation so far. The ninth, Tacit Expertise & Embodied Judgment, questions whether the methodology's assumption that behavior can usually be traced through explicit reasoning is incomplete for expert/intuitive judgment. The tenth, Recognition as an Active Discovery Phenomenon, proposes that "that feels like Beto" may function as a signal during discovery itself, not just the eventual outcome — held as a competing hypothesis against confirmation bias (§1), per founder direction, until distinguishing criteria are found. The eleventh, Rhythms of Living, proposes that stable identity is sometimes expressed through recognizable rhythms (e.g. work-to-restoration in the evening) rather than any single essential behavior — flagged as a likely future friction point for the scene-anchored Documentation Convention, which has no obvious way to represent evidence with no single essential instance.
- **`./beto-2026-08-04-methodology-stress-test.md`** (Discovery Session 2) — two further methodological discoveries, this time about the discipline's own artifacts rather than about Beto's character content. The twelfth: the Discovery Memo (raw research record) and the Character Architecture Reference (distilled architecture) are two distinct artifacts, not one — Experiment 2 had begun conflating them. The thirteenth: the Circumstances Filter — "if the current life circumstances disappeared, would this still describe the character?" — catching a real drafting error where a season of Beto's life ("rebuilds through quiet daily faithfulness") was nearly promoted into an architectural principle. Also records a Founder Decision on sequencing: the Character Architecture structure itself stays unchanged for now; Beto's discovery proceeds — identify enduring principles, stress-test blind spots, then draft the Reference — rather than modifying the structure preemptively from one experiment's early friction.
- **`./beto-2026-08-05-methodology-stress-test.md`** (Discovery Session 3) — four more observations, surfaced while actually organizing Checkpoint 1 into the two-artifact structure. The fourteenth: adjacent candidate principles repeatedly collapsing into one deeper organizing principle, reinforcing Session 1 §5. The fifteenth: a Root/Branch hypothesis about hierarchical principle structure — explicitly flagged as hypothesis only, not methodology. The sixteenth: distinguishing admiration (evidence of what Beto values) from personal practice (evidence of what he actually does) as different evidentiary strengths. The seventeenth: distinguishing Rhythms from isolated Supporting Scenes, extending §11 — and putting the proposed parallel Rhythm format to its first practical test in the Reference Draft itself.
- **`./beto-discovery-memo.md`** — the raw research record for Checkpoint 1: Attention, Human Dignity, the still-open Relational Orientation question, Rhythms, Growth Edge, per-relationship Recognition material, Life Context, and Beto-specific open questions. Distinct from the Reference Draft per the Session 2 Founder Decision on artifact separation.
- **`./beto-character-architecture-reference-DRAFT-checkpoint-1.md`** — the distilled architectural document, two candidate principles (Attention; Human Dignity, with two live framings preserved rather than resolved), a first practical test of the proposed Rhythm format, and Growth Edge material provisionally placed in Recognition Portrait pending a structural decision. No Character Compass yet — judged premature on two principles.

## What this experiment is testing

Two things simultaneously:

1. **About Beto:** whether a psychologically coherent, generative character can be discovered for someone substantially different from Sofía, using the same underlying process.
2. **About Character Architecture:** whether the process itself is genuinely repeatable, or whether Experiment 1's apparent success depended on something specific to Sofía or to how she was discovered. This experiment is the first opportunity to test essentially every open question in `../open-questions.md`.

## Current role in the research program

Beto is the program's second data point and its first attempt at replication. Per `../evidence-index.md`, every existing discovery is rated low-to-moderate confidence specifically because it hasn't been replicated — this experiment is where that replication either starts happening or doesn't.
