# Beto Pérez — Discovery Session 2: Methodology Stress-Test (2026-08-04)

**Status: Working methodology only.** Continues numbering from Discovery Session 1 (`./beto-2026-08-02-methodology-stress-test.md`, observations 1–11, plus the AI/Human Role Division Founder Decision). None of the observations below are founder-approved canon.

## Context

Before drafting Beto's Character Architecture Reference, Jamie & Solara paused to review Sofía's Reference structure for comparison. That pause surfaced two real methodological discoveries — about the discipline itself, not about Beto specifically — judged important enough to resolve before continuing, since both affect every future character, not just this one.

## 12. Discovery Memo and Character Architecture Reference Are Two Distinct Artifacts

**Observation:** Experiment 2 had been drifting toward combining two different things into one document — an internal research record (observations, evidence patterns, methodological discoveries, tensions, emerging hypotheses, confidence levels, open questions) and the distilled architectural document parallel to Sofía's Reference (Principles, Recognition Portrait, Character Compass). Recognized as a mistake before it compounded, not after.

**Proposed methodology:** keep these as two separate artifacts for every future experiment.

- **Discovery Memo** — the raw research record. Observations, evidence, tensions, hypotheses, confidence, open questions, exactly as they occur, not yet distilled.
- **Character Architecture Reference** — the distilled architectural document, following the existing structure established by Sofía's Reference (Reference Metadata → Section I Principles → Recognition Portrait → Character Compass → Closing).

**Connection to existing research (not a new distinction, a mapping onto one already established):** this maps cleanly onto the Discovery/Preservation working observation from `../research-log.md` (2026-08-01) — the Discovery Memo is the Discovery half's raw record; the Character Architecture Reference is the Preservation half's distilled output. The two-artifact structure is arguably what that earlier observation was already implying, made concrete now that a second experiment forced the question.

**Status:** working methodology, proposed to apply to all future experiments, not yet tested beyond this realization.

---

## 13. The Circumstances Filter — Distinguishing Architecture from Season

**Observation:** while drafting candidate principles for Beto, a life circumstance got accidentally promoted into an architectural principle. Example: *"Rebuilds through quiet daily faithfulness."* On reflection, this isn't an enduring trait of who Beto is — it's a season of his life, tied to whatever he's currently rebuilding from. Recognized as a real error in the drafting process, not a hypothetical risk.

**Where it actually belongs:** context, trajectory, or recognition material — Research Context or Recognition Portrait in the existing structure — not Section I.

**Proposed filter:**

> If the current life circumstances disappeared, would this still describe the character?
>
> If yes → candidate architectural principle.
> If no → it likely belongs in context, trajectory, or recognition rather than the enduring architecture.

**Why this matters beyond Beto:** Section I of the Documentation Convention (`../README.md`) has never had an explicit test for whether a candidate statement is even principle-*shaped* — only for how principles, once identified, should be evidenced (Foundational Scene, Supporting Scenes, Interpretation). This fills that gap. A statement can be perfectly well-evidenced with real scenes and still not belong in Section I if what it's actually describing is circumstantial rather than enduring.

**Status:** working methodology, proposed as a refinement to the Documentation Convention. Not yet tested against a case where the answer is genuinely ambiguous — every character will eventually produce a borderline case where "would this survive changed circumstances" isn't obviously yes or no.

## Founder Decision: Sequencing for the Rest of Experiment 2

**Decision:** the Character Architecture structure itself is not being changed right now. Beto's discovery process continues to use it as-is, deliberately, to pressure-test whether the existing framework remains sufficient — rather than modifying the structure preemptively based on one experiment's early friction.

**Sequence going forward:**
1. Identify Beto's enduring architectural principles (applying the Circumstances Filter above).
2. Stress-test blind spots and growth edges against those principles.
3. Only then draft the Character Architecture Reference.

**Framing, preserved as stated:** "Because it's a founder decision about the discipline. It affects every future character. The specific content of Beto can wait another hour. The methodology shouldn't." This is why this document exists before Beto's actual content does.

**Status:** Founder Decision on process, not on structure. The structure remains exactly as established through Sofía; whether it needs to evolve stays an open, evidence-driven question rather than something decided ahead of the evidence.
