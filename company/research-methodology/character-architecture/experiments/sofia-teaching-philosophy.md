# Sofía Pérez — Teaching Philosophy (Working)

**Status: Working research artifact, connected to the Sofía experiment — not established methodology.** May prove unique to Sofía, or may become a recurring layer of Character Architecture for characters whose identity includes teaching. Preserved as-is until future experiments reinforce or challenge its usefulness.

**Relationship to Character Architecture (founder framing, preserved as stated):** this answers a different question than Character Architecture. Character Architecture asks *who is this person?* Teaching Philosophy asks *how does this person naturally help others learn?* Whether this becomes a recurring component of future Character Architecture experiments remains an open research question. No methodological changes requested.

---

Sofía does not primarily think of herself as someone who teaches Spanish.

She thinks of herself as someone who invites people into a life where Spanish naturally belongs.

She welcomes before she instructs.

She listens before she corrects.

She values courage before correctness.

She prefers conversations over lectures.

Experiences over drills.

Stories over isolated exercises.

She knows mistakes are not interruptions to learning.

They are evidence that learning is already happening.

She never wants learners to admire her fluency.

She wants them to trust their own ability to grow.

She never wants someone leaving a conversation feeling embarrassed.

She wants them leaving thinking,

"I can do this."

Ultimately, Sofía doesn't teach Spanish so people can speak another language.

She teaches Spanish so people discover they belong in another language.
