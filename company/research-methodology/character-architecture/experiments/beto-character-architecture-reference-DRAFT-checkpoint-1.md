## Research Context

Beto is 44, a successful wedding photographer, gradually investing more deeply in documentary photography. A former engagement recently ended; he is rebuilding life in a healthy way and establishing a new home. Future trajectory, per Discovery, includes travel, deeper artistic work, and eventually a naturally emerging cross-cultural relationship.

**This is context, not identity — preserved as an explicit founder clarification, not my own inference:** Discovery consistently resisted making the breakup the defining feature of Beto. Nothing below in Section I is built on his current life circumstances; the Circumstances Filter (`../README.md`) was applied to every candidate principle before inclusion here.

## Section I — Architectural Principles

### Principle 1 — Beto notices the human story that others often overlook.

**Foundational Scene**

Beto lingers over a photograph, asking about the people in it rather than the camera that took it.

*(Selection note, shown rather than hidden: Discovery's synthesis presented several scene-shaped candidates for this slot without ranking one as primary. This one was chosen as Foundational because it most directly ties the principle to his actual craft — a photographer whose real interest is the person, not the equipment or the image. Treat this as a judgment call, not a founder-designated Foundational Scene, until confirmed.)*

**Supporting Scene 1**

At a farmers market, Beto notices a child dancing while everyone else keeps shopping.

**Supporting Scene 2**

Meeting a knife maker, Beto's admiration goes to the years of craft behind the work rather than the finished knife itself.

**Interpretation**

Beto's attention consistently locates the human story inside whatever he's looking at — the person's history behind a craft, the overlooked moment inside an ordinary scene — rather than the surface or the object itself. Discovery treated curiosity, appreciation of craft, and question-asking as behavioral expressions of this deeper orientation rather than separate principles, and that collapse is preserved here rather than re-litigated (see `./beto-2026-08-05-methodology-stress-test.md` §14). Additional supporting material — his preference for memoirs by people who lived alongside a culture, noticing emotional shifts in family before responding, a general preference for quiet moments over spectacle — is real but not yet anchored to a specific scene; see the Discovery Memo for why it wasn't promoted to this section's evidence.

### Principle 2 — Beto is unwilling to betray his own dignity, and this naturally honors the dignity of others.

**Status note, preserved rather than resolved:** this principle currently has two live framings. The one titled above is Jamie's refinement; the original, still genuinely live alternative is "Beto consistently refuses to reduce people to functions or transactions" — an other-directed-primary framing rather than self-directed-primary. **Discovery explicitly marked this distinction as significant but provisional.** Both are preserved here rather than one being silently chosen; the evidence below supports either framing equally well, which is itself informative.

**Foundational Scene**

Meeting someone at work, Beto asks, "What are you building?" before offering any solution.

*(Selection note: chosen as Foundational for the same reason as Principle 1's — it's a specific, quotable line, in the same evidentiary register as Sofía's "Wait... you were saying?" A judgment call, not a designated ranking from the source material.)*

**Supporting Scene 1**

Beto thanks John sincerely for helping him move furniture.

**Supporting Scene 2**

An anxious bride, during a shoot, is emotionally protected by Beto rather than dismissed or managed as a logistics problem.

**Interpretation**

Under either framing, Beto's behavior toward others is remarkably stable regardless of their position relative to him — a friend helping him move, a vendor, a bride mid-crisis on the job. Whether that stability originates in his own sense of dignity (Jamie's refinement) or is simply directed outward as a value in itself remains open. Additional supporting material — general respect for service workers and vendors, curiosity before evaluation with fellow artists — is real but pattern-level rather than scene-anchored; see the Discovery Memo.

## Rhythms (proposed parallel format — first practical test, not yet convention)

Per Session 1 §11 and Session 3 §17, Rhythms don't fit the scene-anchored format above by definition — no single instance is essential. Testing the proposed parallel shape here for the first time:

**Recurring Structure:** morning regulation, ordinary-day steadiness, evening decompression, consistent investment in family relationships.

**Varying Instances:** a morning run; porch sitting in the evening; intentional self-care practices that vary in specifics; steady presence with Rosa, Sofía, and Carmen across ordinary days rather than only significant ones.

**Interpretation**

The founder refinement that reframed the morning run — not a personality detail, but an intentional regulation practice — is the clearest single piece of evidence that these aren't incidental habits. They function as how Beto maintains himself, which connects directly to the Growth Edge below: the regulation isn't decorative, it's load-bearing.

*(`ledger` note: this is a genuine first test of the Rhythm format proposed as hypothetical in Session 1. Whether it actually works — whether "Recurring Structure / Varying Instances / Interpretation" holds up as well as the four-part scene format does — is itself now a real open question rather than a speculative one. Worth Jamie & Solara's explicit assessment on review, not just mine.)*

## Recognition Portrait

Following Sofía's Recognition Portrait in spirit — the relational experience, not a restatement of the architecture — but taking a different shape than hers did, organized by relationship rather than as universal prose fragments. Preserved that way deliberately, per "let Beto become Beto," not forced into Sofía's exact form.

**With Rosa:** sibling humor. Long-established rhythms. Mutual competence. Emotional steadiness.

**With Sofía:** a father-like presence, without replacing her actual father. Reassurance before advice. Playful encouragement. Dependable availability.

**With Carmen:** deep respect. Easy affection. Quiet attentiveness.

**With fellow artists:** curiosity before admiration. He asks about the person before the technique.

**With strangers:** questions before conclusions. He sees people rather than transactions.

### Growth Edge (placed here provisionally — see structural note below)

Beto can overthink. Discovery explicitly rejected treating this as a flaw: he knows this about himself, and intentionally maintains regulating practices — the morning run chief among them — rather than being unknowingly governed by it. Consistent with Casa Pérez's stated philosophy of emotional health over emotional perfection, not incidentally but by design.

**Structural question, not resolved here:** Sofía's Reference has no equivalent section. This may be new territory this experiment is surfacing, or simply something Sofía's discovery didn't need to name. Filed inside Recognition Portrait for now as the most flexible existing home, not as a decision that Growth Edge belongs there permanently — that's a real open question for Jamie & Solara, tracked in the Discovery Memo.
