# Open Questions — Character Architecture

Unresolved research questions, treated as valuable research assets — not gaps to be embarrassed about or rushed to close.

- Does the process replicate across fundamentally different characters?
- Which discoveries are universal versus character-specific?
- Does "attention precedes dialogue" generalize?
- At what point does a character become generative?
- Which parts require human judgment?
- Which parts could eventually be software-assisted?
- What evidence would falsify current hypotheses?
- **Is Character Architecture actually two distinct disciplines — Discovery ("how do we recognize a person?") and Preservation ("how do we preserve a discovered person with enough fidelity that future collaborators recognize the same person years later?") — or is this a false split?** (2026-08-01, see `./research-log.md`). Now being tested: Experiment 2 (Beto), Discovery Active as of 2026-08-02.
- **Does the program's Proposed Working Thesis hold beyond Sofía** — that Character Architecture is the discipline of discovering and preserving human identity with enough fidelity that an AI can participate in long-term relationships while remaining recognizably faithful to the identity it embodies? (2026-08-01, see `../README.md`). Now being tested: Experiment 2 (Beto), Discovery Active as of 2026-08-02.
- **Does the Proposed Character Architecture Experiment Lifecycle hold across experiments** — Experiment Created → Discovery Active → Discovery Complete → Reference Established → Stewardship → (New Evidence Emerges) → Reference Updated? Observed only through Sofía so far; the New Evidence/Reference Updated loop hasn't been tested at all. (2026-08-02, see `./research-log.md`). Now being tested: Experiment 2 (Beto), Discovery Active as of 2026-08-02.
- **Does "characters teach because they love differently" hold beyond Sofía** — i.e. does teaching style consistently fall out of a completed Character Architecture reference, rather than needing separate design? Observed only through Sofía so far (see `./research-log.md`'s 2026-08-02 cross-program convergence entry). Now being tested: Experiment 2 (Beto), Discovery Active as of 2026-08-02. *(Added retroactively — this question should have been logged alongside the observation itself; flagged as a process gap during a 2026-08-02 pressure-test review.)*
- **Does Teaching Philosophy generalize as a recurring Character Architecture layer, or is it specific to Sofía?** Sofía's Teaching Philosophy (`./experiments/sofia-teaching-philosophy.md`) answers a different question than Character Architecture itself (*how does this person naturally help others learn?* vs. *who is this person?*) — unclear whether this becomes a recurring component of future references, or only applies to characters whose identity includes teaching. Also unresolved: whether its resonance with existing Casa Pérez instructional-philosophy material (`casa-perez/projects/agrupa/master-reference-v1.4.md` §3, `casa-perez/FOUNDATION.md`, Agrupa's DP-009) reflects Sofía's philosophy being downstream of that material, or an independent rediscovery of the same values. (2026-08-02, see `./research-log.md`). Now being tested: Experiment 2 (Beto), Discovery Active as of 2026-08-02.

- **Does "an organizing principle may explain behavior but must never replace it" hold as a durable safeguard against confirmation bias?** Surfaced during Beto's first discovery session (2026-08-02, see `./research-log.md` and `./experiments/beto-2026-08-02-methodology-stress-test.md`) after his outward-orientation principle drifted through several increasingly reductive readings (observer → humble artist → anti-recognition → uncomfortable receiving praise) before founder correction. Not yet tested against a third character.
- **Does the Occupation / Calling / Orientation distinction generalize** — and does it apply retroactively to Sofía, whose Reference v1.0 didn't distinguish these dimensions explicitly? (2026-08-02, Beto Discovery Session 1).
- **Does "organizing principles must be earned" (Behavior → Patterns → Organizing Principles, never assumed early) hold, and should the Documentation Convention eventually require a minimum number of independent supporting observations before a Principle can be recorded?** (2026-08-02, Beto Discovery Session 1). Would be a real change to the Documentation Convention if it holds — not proposed yet.
- **Should "founder corrections are first-class research data" become a formal practice (e.g. a dedicated corrections log per experiment), or stay informal?** (2026-08-02, Beto Discovery Session 1) — the observation itself is that founder corrections during Beto's session revealed methodology failure modes more clearly than the character discoveries did.
- **Should Character Architecture intentionally alternate between horizontal inquiry (breadth — more scenes, relationships, behaviors) and vertical inquiry (depth — origins, meaning, worldview, formation) throughout discovery, or should vertical inquiry only occur after a stable pattern has emerged?** (2026-08-02, Beto Discovery Session 1, see `./research-log.md` and `./experiments/beto-2026-08-02-methodology-stress-test.md` §8.) Surfaced when discovery explored Beto's dream of becoming a traveling photographer without asking *why* it became his dream — the founder volunteered that origin unprompted, and it turned out to be the deeper discovery. Flagged as possibly the most significant methodological observation so far; not yet tested across additional sessions.
- **Does Character Architecture read more like biography than diagnosis across future experiments** — understanding how multiple coherent aspects coexist in one person, rather than finding "the one thing that explains this person"? Founder Direction from Beto Discovery Session 1 (2026-08-02), flagged as a possible defining philosophical shift for the discipline. Not yet tested beyond this one session.

**Standing rule:** a question moves off this list only when evidence answers it (recorded in `./evidence-index.md`) — not when it stops feeling urgent or gets superseded by newer questions. If a question is abandoned rather than answered, record why here rather than silently dropping it.
