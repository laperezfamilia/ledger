# Open Questions — Character Architecture

Unresolved research questions, treated as valuable research assets — not gaps to be embarrassed about or rushed to close.

- Does the process replicate across fundamentally different characters?
- Which discoveries are universal versus character-specific?
- Does "attention precedes dialogue" generalize?
- At what point does a character become generative?
- Which parts require human judgment?
- Which parts could eventually be software-assisted?
- What evidence would falsify current hypotheses?
- **Is Character Architecture actually two distinct disciplines — Discovery ("how do we recognize a person?") and Preservation ("how do we preserve a discovered person with enough fidelity that future collaborators recognize the same person years later?") — or is this a false split?** (2026-08-01, see `./research-log.md`). First real test: Experiment 2 (Beto).
- **Does the program's Proposed Working Thesis hold beyond Sofía** — that Character Architecture is the discipline of discovering and preserving human identity with enough fidelity that an AI can participate in long-term relationships while remaining recognizably faithful to the identity it embodies? (2026-08-01, see `../README.md`). First real test: Experiment 2 (Beto).
- **Does the Proposed Character Architecture Experiment Lifecycle hold across experiments** — Experiment Created → Discovery Active → Discovery Complete → Reference Established → Stewardship → (New Evidence Emerges) → Reference Updated? Observed only through Sofía so far; the New Evidence/Reference Updated loop hasn't been tested at all. (2026-08-02, see `./research-log.md`). First real test: Experiment 2 (Beto).
- **Does "characters teach because they love differently" hold beyond Sofía** — i.e. does teaching style consistently fall out of a completed Character Architecture reference, rather than needing separate design? Observed only through Sofía so far (see `./research-log.md`'s 2026-08-02 cross-program convergence entry). First real test: Experiment 2 (Beto). *(Added retroactively — this question should have been logged alongside the observation itself; flagged as a process gap during a 2026-08-02 pressure-test review.)*
- **Does Teaching Philosophy generalize as a recurring Character Architecture layer, or is it specific to Sofía?** Sofía's Teaching Philosophy (`./experiments/sofia-teaching-philosophy.md`) answers a different question than Character Architecture itself (*how does this person naturally help others learn?* vs. *who is this person?*) — unclear whether this becomes a recurring component of future references, or only applies to characters whose identity includes teaching. Also unresolved: whether its resonance with existing Casa Pérez instructional-philosophy material (`casa-perez/projects/agrupa/master-reference-v1.4.md` §3, `casa-perez/FOUNDATION.md`, Agrupa's DP-009) reflects Sofía's philosophy being downstream of that material, or an independent rediscovery of the same values. (2026-08-02, see `./research-log.md`). First real test: Experiment 2 (Beto).

**Standing rule:** a question moves off this list only when evidence answers it (recorded in `./evidence-index.md`) — not when it stops feeling urgent or gets superseded by newer questions. If a question is abandoned rather than answered, record why here rather than silently dropping it.
