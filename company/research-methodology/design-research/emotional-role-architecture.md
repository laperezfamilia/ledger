# Emotional Role Architecture (Working Thesis)

**Status: Working Thesis, not canon.** Part of the Design Research program (`./README.md`) — a discovery from the 2026-08-02 session, to be tested through implementation and observation.

## The thesis

Products do not simply occupy markets. They occupy emotional roles within people's lives.

## Examples observed across products

| Product | Emotional role |
|---|---|
| Speak | Patient instructor |
| Finch | Caring companion |
| Spotify | Taste curator |
| Apple Photos | Memory keeper |
| WhatsApp | Conversation space |
| Casa Pérez | Adopted family |

## Current Casa Pérez thinking

| Product | Emotional role |
|---|---|
| Find the Words | Trusted preparation partner |
| Sofía | Older sister who introduces learners to the family |
| Casa Pérez Group Chat | Adopted family |
| ¡Agrupa! | Family game night |

## The goal

Every product should have one clear relational identity — a single emotional role a person could name if asked "what is this to you?"

*(`ledger` note: connects to `./adopted-family-design-filter.md` — once a product's emotional role is named, the design filter is one operational way to test whether decisions stay faithful to it.)*
