# Emotional Role Architecture (Working Thesis)

**Status: Working Thesis, not canon.** Part of the Design Research program (`./README.md`) — a discovery from the 2026-08-02 session, to be tested through implementation and observation.

## The thesis

Products do not simply occupy markets. They occupy emotional roles within people's lives.

## Examples observed across products

| Product | Emotional role |
|---|---|
| Speak | Patient instructor |
| Finch | Caring companion |
| Spotify | Taste curator |
| Apple Photos | Memory keeper |
| WhatsApp | Conversation space |
| Casa Pérez | Adopted family |

## Current Casa Pérez thinking

| Product | Emotional role |
|---|---|
| Find the Words | Trusted preparation partner |
| Sofía | Older sister who introduces learners to the family |
| Casa Pérez Group Chat | Adopted family |
| ¡Agrupa! | Family game night |

## The goal

Every product should have one clear relational identity — a single emotional role a person could name if asked "what is this to you?"

## Clarification (Founder-approved, 2026-08-02): this describes products, not characters

A pressure-test review raised a real risk worth naming directly: Character Architecture's whole documentation convention exists to resist flattening a person into a single adjective ("Sofía is warm" drifts; a scene doesn't). "One clear relational identity per product" could read as doing the same flattening move one layer up.

**That is not the intent, and the table above should be read accordingly.** Sofía (the character) remains a rich, multidimensional person — that richness is exactly what Character Architecture exists to discover and preserve, and nothing here changes that. "Older sister who introduces learners to the family" describes **the Sofía *product*'s relational role** — a property of the product relationship, not a reduction of the character herself. The same character can generate many product surfaces; the role belongs to the surface, not to the person underneath it.

**The resulting layering, consistent with `./adopted-family-design-filter.md`'s revision:**

> Family philosophy → Character Architecture → Product behavior

Emotional Role Architecture names the product-facing output of that pipeline. It should never be used as a substitute for, or a summary of, a character's actual Character Architecture reference.

*(`ledger` note: connects to `./adopted-family-design-filter.md` — once a product's emotional role is named, the two-step filter (family value → character-specific expression) is one operational way to test whether decisions stay faithful to it.)*
