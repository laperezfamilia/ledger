# Design Research

**Status: Discovery Artifacts / Working Theses — not canon.** Recommended by Jamie & Solara to be preserved as such, tested through implementation, observation, and future design work rather than adopted outright.

**Research Question:** What human truths do existing products reveal, and how would Casa Pérez naturally express those same truths in its own way?

## What changed (2026-08-02)

Design Research has matured from casual inspiration-gathering into its own discipline. Products are no longer studied primarily to find features worth having — they're studied to understand the human problem a product solves, the human truth it reveals, and how Casa Pérez would naturally express that same truth differently.

## Proposed Research Framework

For every product studied:

1. What did they build?
2. Why did they build it?
3. What human need/problem does it serve?
4. What evidence supports that interpretation?
5. How would Casa Pérez naturally express that principle?
6. Where does it belong in our ecosystem?
7. Why are we intentionally different?

**Worked example — Speak:**

- **Feature:** Speech recognition, real instructor videos, AI conversation.
- **Human problem:** People are afraid to speak incorrectly.
- **Casa Pérez translation:** Rather than reducing fear through drills alone, Sofía reduces fear through relationship, gentle correction, and psychological safety.

The feature may differ. The human problem remains. **The deliverable of this research is never a feature list — it's human problems, human feelings, human principles, and the Casa Pérez translation of each.**

## Products currently under study

Speak, Finch, WhatsApp, Spotify, Unedo, ELSA, Dreaming Spanish, Language Transfer. This list is expected to grow; membership on it is not itself a finding.

## Founder Clarification: "The work is the research"

Casa Pérez is not becoming a research institute that occasionally builds products. It builds products, and the products become the place where learning happens.

**Working loop:** Observe → Build → Watch → Learn → Build Better.

Research serves craftsmanship. Craftsmanship does not serve research.

*(`ledger` note: this loop resonates with `../../governance/operating-model.md`'s four-phase team workflow and shared principles — related, not merged; that document governs how the team collaborates, this one governs how products get studied.)*

## Working directional distinction (2026-08-02)

> Casa Pérez is not trying to discover better ways to teach Spanish. Casa Pérez is trying to discover better ways for humans to become comfortable living part of their lives in another language.

Reported as currently influencing nearly every product decision. Preserved here as a working distinction, not yet canon.

## Company Mission — current strongest wording (2026-08-02, working)

> We build products. But the products are not the end. The products are instruments for discovering enduring principles about how humans connect across languages and cultures. Every product teaches us something.

Jamie & Solara flagged this as the strongest articulation of long-term company direction so far. **Status: Working Thesis, not canon.**
