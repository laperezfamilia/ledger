# The Adopted Family Design Filter (Working Thesis)

**Status: Working Thesis, not canon.** Part of the Design Research program (`./README.md`). Flagged by Jamie & Solara as potentially the single biggest operational discovery from the 2026-08-02 session.

## The filter

For major product decisions, ask:

> What would an adopted family do?

This is becoming an operational design filter, not merely a metaphor — a question to actually run product decisions through, alongside (not replacing) existing tests like `casa-perez/FOUNDATION.md`'s Hospitality Test ("does this welcome first?").

## Examples

**Notifications**

- Instead of: *"Your streak is ending."*
- Think — Rosa: *"We've missed having breakfast with you."*

**Corrections**

- Instead of: *"Incorrect."*
- Think — Sofía: *"I know exactly what you meant. Can I show you how I'd probably say it?"*

**Returning after two weeks**

- Instead of: *"Welcome back!"*
- Think: *"Life gets busy. Come sit down and tell us what's been going on."*

## Related principle: products naturally exist inside the world

A second, closely related discovery from the same session: products should appear inside Casa Pérez because they genuinely belong in the characters' lives — not because they're being marketed.

**Natural:**
- Beto watches Sofía's newest YouTube video while waiting in an airport.
- Rosa prints one of Sofía's Etsy resources for work.
- Carmen proudly shares something Sofía made with a friend.

**Forced:**
- Characters interrupt a conversation simply to recommend an app.

**Pressure test:**

> If our products disappeared tomorrow, would this conversation still feel authentic?

If yes, it belongs.

*(`ledger` note: this pressure test and the "what would an adopted family do?" filter are two angles on the same discovery — the family's world should feel real independent of the products in it, and product decisions should feel like something the family would actually do. Kept in one file because they were discovered together and are meant to be applied together, not because they're literally the same claim.)*
