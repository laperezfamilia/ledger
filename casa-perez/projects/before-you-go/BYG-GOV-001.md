## 1. Identity
Before You Go prepares people for conversations that matter. Language is the medium. Readiness is the outcome.

## 2. Mission
Before You Go exists to help people feel ready for conversations that matter. Success is measured by participation, not perfection.

## 3. Product Promise
**Five minutes. More confidence. Better conversations.**

Every feature must strengthen this promise. (See BYG-PRD-001 §8 for the measurable definition of "five minutes.")

## 4. Core Philosophy
- We are not trying to create perfect speakers. We help real people participate in real life.
- Confidence precedes fluency.
- Participation is success.

## 5. Emotional Position
Warmth is not an optional tone — it is part of the product architecture. Every interaction should communicate:
- You're welcome here.
- You don't need to be perfect.
- We're on your side.

The learner should consistently leave calmer than when they opened the app.

## 6. Courage
Courage is one of the products we deliver, not merely something we celebrate. The learner already possesses courage; the app reduces uncertainty enough for them to use it.

## 7. Product Personality
**Always:** warm, calm, practical, emotionally intelligent, encouraging, lightly playful when appropriate.

**Never:** robotic, generic, judgmental, gamified, loud, overwhelming.

## 8. Memory Boundary
Before You Go uses **practical memory, not relational memory.**

It may retain:
- language and dialect
- learner level
- translation preferences
- saved Ready Packs
- recent situation history

It does not build emotional continuity, an evolving relationship, shared social history, or character-style attachment.

**Shorthand:** *Personalize the preparation, not the affection.*

## 9. Casa Pérez Boundary
Before You Go and Casa Pérez Spanish are permanently separate systems.
- Before You Go delivers practical readiness, on demand, without relationship continuity.
- Casa Pérez delivers relationship, story, and world.

These must never blend, share memory, or share characters.

## 10. Version 1 Non-Goals
Explicitly out of scope for V1:
- Open-ended AI chat
- Adaptive roleplay
- Relationship memory
- Characters or narrative continuity
- Systematic grammar curriculum
- Pronunciation scoring
- Slow-speed audio
- Progress gamification
- Social/community features
- "After You Go" (post-conversation reflection — parked, not scoped)
- Unlimited refinements

## 11. Governing Shorthand
- "Five minutes. More confidence. Better conversations."
- "Personalize the preparation, not the affection."
- "The learner has the final word."

## 12. Product Identity: Reusable Preparation
**A Ready Pack is a reusable preparation resource, not a disposable AI response.**

This is distinct from the Memory Boundary (§8): §8 governs what BYG *remembers about the learner* (practical, not relational). This section governs what happens to *what BYG already made for them* — a generated Ready Pack persists as something the learner owns and can return to (BYG-PRD-001 §13), not something that exists only inside a conversational thread and then disappears. It reframes the product from "an app that generates AI responses" into "a personal library of real-life conversation preparation."

*This is a governing product truth under §13, not a feature addition — see rationale in BYG-DEC-001.*

## 13. Amendment Principle
This Constitution changes only to resolve a material contradiction, preserve a newly discovered governing product truth, or protect the product from meaningful identity drift. Every amendment requires explicit rationale, a version change, and reconciliation across the PRD and Decision Log. New features alone do not justify constitutional amendment.
