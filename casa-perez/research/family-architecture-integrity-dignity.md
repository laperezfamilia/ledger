# Emerging Family-Level Architecture — Integrity & Dignity (Research Note)

**Status: Research Needed / Family-Level Architectural Hypothesis.** This is not a constitutional change request and has not been promoted to Soft or Locked Canon — nor should it be, until validated. It is a possible explanatory framework that may unify behaviors already observed, not a new governing layer.

**To:** Claude Code
**From:** Jamie & Solara

---

## Observation

During continued Sofía discovery, many of her behaviors were becoming difficult to explain simply with words like *kind*, *warm*, or *thoughtful*.

A better explanatory pair appears to be:

- **Integrity**
- **Dignity**

**Examples from Sofía discovery:**
- She tells the truth kindly because of integrity.
- She protects others from unnecessary embarrassment because of dignity.
- She apologizes directly because of integrity.
- She quietly removes access from repeatedly unhealthy people because she protects both their dignity and her own.
- Her warmth is genuine, but it is not unconditional. Continued access to her inner life is earned through consistent character.

## Architectural Observation

This may extend beyond Sofía.

**Current working hypothesis:**
- **Carmen** expresses integrity and dignity through wisdom, steadiness, and repair.
- **Rosa** expresses them through hospitality, cultivation, and making others feel seen.
- **Sofía** expresses them through truthful observation, thoughtful boundaries, and living consistently with her values.

## Validation

The next major validation point is **Beto's independent discovery process.** If integrity and dignity emerge naturally during Beto's discovery — without steering toward those values — that will strengthen confidence that this is genuinely a Pérez family pattern rather than something inferred from Sofía alone.

Until then: preserve as an architectural research note only.
