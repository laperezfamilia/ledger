## Overview

The Casa Pérez Foundation is the philosophical and governance layer beneath every Casa Pérez product.

It is not a product itself.

It exists to answer questions like:

* What are we building?
* Why are we building it?
* How should future decisions be made?
* What principles are immutable?
* What is canon versus experimentation?

Many of these ideas currently exist across conversation history rather than a single consolidated document. This README summarizes Solara's understanding of that structure.

## Expected Sections

### 1. Vision

**Jamie's refined framing (this revision, preferred over the original below):**

> Casa Pérez is building a world people want to belong to. Spanish is the language spoken in that world.

**Original framing (Solara's draft, preserved for the record — superseded by the line above but not erased):**

> Casa Pérez is not primarily a language-learning company. It is a hospitality company whose medium happens to be Spanish.

Belonging comes before instruction. Relationship comes before curriculum. Learning emerges naturally from participation.

### 2. Core Founder Principle

Most language products follow:

Learn → Practice → Improve → Belong

Casa Pérez intentionally reverses that.

Belong → Relax → Engage → Learn

This principle should remain foundational.

### 3. Hospitality Test

Whenever multiple valid design options exist, choose the one that feels more welcoming.

Internal shorthand:

"Does this welcome first?"

This became one of the primary governance tests.

### 4. Product Philosophy

The products should never feel like educational software disguised as entertainment.

Instead they should feel like participating in ordinary life with ordinary people.

Education is intentionally embedded rather than foregrounded.

### 5. The Conversation Company

One major architectural realization during development:

The conversation is not supporting the product.

The conversation is the product.

Language learning is the outcome.

Relationships are the experience.

### 6. Ordinary Life Engine

Characters should generate ordinary human interactions.

Not plots.

Not scripted drama.

The benchmark became:

Could this naturally happen if these people were real?

### 7. Humanity Preservation

Characters should never become collections of traits.

Instead:

Treat them as genuine people whose personalities naturally express themselves through everyday decisions.

The design goal became preserving human personhood while translating characters into computational systems.

### 8. Character Architecture

Current known core family:

* Carmen
* Rosa

* Sofía

Characters are developed through layered architecture rather than simple biographies.

Layers 1–2 are considered foundational.

### 9. Educational Philosophy

Education should disappear into conversation.

Grammar serves communication.

Conversation serves relationship.

Relationship serves belonging.

### 11. Design Language

Recurring principles include:

* Warmth over efficiency.
* Invitation over obligation.
* Gentle correction.
* Emotional safety.
* Curiosity over performance.
* Real conversations over artificial lessons.
