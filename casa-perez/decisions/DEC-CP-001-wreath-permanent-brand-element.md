# DEC-CP-001 — Wreath as a Permanent Brand Element

**Provenance note:** transcribed from `Casa Pérez branding decision memo.png`, located by Jamie in her File Library. Source image preserved at `../brand/assets/branding-decision-memo-wreath.png`.

**To:** Claude
**From:** Solara (Creative Director / Brand Steward)
**Re:** Casa Pérez Foundation Decisions — Wreath Recommendation & Next Steps

---

> Thank you for raising the important question about the wreath. After carefully reviewing both versions and considering Claude's concern about seasonal association, here is my recommendation and rationale.

## Decision

**Keep the wreath.** Not because it's decorative, but because it transforms the doorway from a symbol into a home.

## Why the Wreath Strengthens the Brand

1. **It says "Someone lives here."** It suggests care, intention, and a lived-in home, not just a building.
2. **It enhances recognition.** That circular element on the door becomes part of the visual memory at any size.
3. **It supports the brand promise.** It visually communicates warmth, welcome, and belonging.

## Addressing the Seasonal Concern

Claude raised the concern that wreaths are often associated with Christmas in the U.S. Resolution: this particular wreath avoids that association because:

- It is foliage only — no ribbons, berries, pinecones, ornaments, or flowers.
- It is restrained, natural, and architectural.
- The rest of the identity is not seasonal.
- It reads as an all-season, permanent element of the home.

## Brand Standards Direction

The wreath is to be defined in Brand Standards as:

> **Permanent handcrafted greenery wreath.**

**Guidelines:**
- Foliage only
- Evergreen
- No flowers
- No bows
- No ornaments
- No seasonal variations
- No holiday adaptations
- Part of the architecture of the home, not seasonal décor

## Effect on the Primary Mark

The locked primary mark (`../brand/branding-style-guide.md`) includes this wreath on the door, per this decision. See that file for the full visual specification.
